# DEC-024 — Fito Gen product module and compact typography

## Implemented scope

- The homepage hero uses the two-word headline `Praktyczna inżynieria`.
- Public H1, H2 and card headings use a smaller responsive scale.
- The Fito Gen landing is presented as a complete product module rather than an internal launch-status page.
- Internal messages about builds, unfinished work and future product-launch steps were removed from public UI.
- The Fito Gen module communicates:
  - offline-first operation,
  - local ownership of operational data,
  - a simple workflow for Polish plant nurseries,
  - updates, news and versioned reference packages delivered by egenlabs.eu.
- The final application download remains a separate release activation step.

## Acceptance checks

- No public Fito Gen section uses `Produkt w przygotowaniu` or `Program zostanie dodany później`.
- The Fito Gen page contains no internal build counters.
- The product page remains available at `/products/fito-gen`.
- The homepage and catalog present Fito Gen as an application product line.
